Privacy Policy

Last updated November 19, 2026

The short version. CalenJob keeps your schedule, customers and quotes in your account so they're on every device you sign in on. We use your information to run the app, never to advertise. We don't sell it, we don't track you across other apps or websites, and the app has no advertising tools. To improve the app, it counts how features are used, anonymously, and you can turn that off.

This policy covers the CalenJob iPhone app, the quote signing pages at calenjob.com, and the calenjob.com website, all run by CalenJob LLC ("CalenJob", "we", "us"). If you use CalenJob for your business, you also enter information about your own customers. That section is below too.

What we collect

Your account

  • Your email address and password (passwords are stored only in protected, scrambled form), or your Apple ID sign-in if you use Sign in with Apple. With Apple, you can choose to share a private relay email instead of your real one.
  • Your name, business name, business phone, business email, business address and service type, if you enter them.
  • Your Home Base and other saved locations.

What you put in the app

  • Jobs and tasks: titles, dates, times, addresses, notes, durations, colors, drive times and mileage.
  • Customers: names, phone numbers, email addresses, addresses, tags and notes.
  • Quotes: line items, prices, terms, and signatures.
  • Follow-up reminders and your app settings.
  • A picture of each day's route map from your Dashboard, so past days still show their route. These are kept for 15 days.

This is saved on your iPhone and in your CalenJob account, so it backs up and appears on your other devices.

Photos and files

Photos you add to jobs and customers, customer attachments, and your quote logo stay on your iPhone. They are not uploaded to your account. The exception: when you send a quote for your customer to sign, the quote, including any photos on it, is uploaded so your customer can open it.

Your location

CalenJob uses your iPhone's location only while you're using the app, and only if you allow it:

  • When you tap Use Current Location, to fill in the address where you are.
  • To show address search results near you.

The app does not track your location in the background and does not keep a history of where you've been. An address only becomes part of your account if you save it to a job, task or location.

Notifications

Job and reminder alerts are scheduled on your iPhone. If you send quotes for signing, we store a notification token for your iPhone so we can tell you when a customer signs. It's removed when you sign out.

Subscriptions

Apple handles subscription payments. We don't receive your card or bank details, only whether your subscription is active.

Feedback when you delete your account

If you answer "Why are you leaving?" when deleting your account, those answers are saved without your name or account, to help us improve CalenJob.

Anonymous usage numbers

To see which features get used and what goes wrong, the app counts things like how many jobs, tasks and reminders are added, how many quotes are sent, how many route optimizations run, which screens are opened, when a usage limit is reached, and problems such as a backup that failed or time without a connection. With those counts it sends your trade, whether you're in your free trial, whether Drive Time Scheduling is on, which permissions (notifications, location, camera, photos) are on, and the app and iOS version.

These numbers are sent once a day under a random ID made by your iPhone. They are not linked to your name, email or account, and they never include customer details, addresses, notes, quote contents or amounts, or anything you type. We keep them for about 13 months. To turn this off, go to Settings > Data > Share Usage Stats.

Your customers' information

When you use CalenJob for your business, you decide what information about your customers to enter. We store and process it only to provide the app to you, and we don't use it for anything else or contact your customers ourselves. You're responsible for having your customers' permission to keep their details and to send them quotes.

When your customer signs a quote

Your customer opens the quote on a calenjob.com page. When they accept, we record:

  • the name they type and their drawn signature
  • which option they accepted and the total
  • the date and time, the consent statement they agreed to, and when they first opened the page
  • a fingerprint of the quote page they saw, so it can be shown later that the page wasn't changed

This is a record of the signature, and you, the business that sent the quote, can see it. We don't use it for anything else.

How we use information

  • To run the app: calculate drive times, show maps, schedule alerts, back up your data and keep your devices in sync.
  • To send quotes for signing and tell you when they're signed.
  • To sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • To answer you when you contact us.
  • To understand, anonymously, which features get used and what goes wrong, so we can improve the app.
  • To keep usage within fair limits (for example, how many route optimizations an account runs each week, and how many address and map requests it makes each day). We keep these daily counts for 60 days.

We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or share it for others to advertise to you.

We may also share information if the law requires it, to protect people's safety or our rights, or as part of a sale or merger of the business. If CalenJob changes owners, this policy continues to apply to your information.

How long we keep it

  • Your account data: while your account is open.
  • After you delete your account: your data is kept for 30 days in case you change your mind (sign back in to restore it), then permanently deleted. Quote pages are taken off the web right away.
  • Quote signing pages: a link expires 60 days after it's sent. If it wasn't signed, the page is deleted 30 days after that. Signed quotes stay with your account. Photos on quote pages older than 6 months are stored at a smaller size.
  • Dashboard route map pictures: 15 days.
  • Anonymous usage numbers: about 13 months.
  • Backups: copies in our hosting provider's backups are replaced on a rolling schedule and are gone within a short time after deletion.

Your choices and rights

  • See and change your information: everything you've entered is in the app, where you can edit or delete it.
  • Delete your account: in the app, go to Profile > Subscription > Delete Account. If you signed in with Apple, this also removes CalenJob from your Apple ID.
  • Get a copy: email us and we'll send you a copy of your account data.
  • Location, photos and notifications: turn these on or off at any time in your iPhone's Settings > CalenJob.

Depending on where you live, you may have other rights under local law, such as asking what we hold about you or asking us to delete it. Email us and we'll help.

If you're a customer of a business that uses CalenJob, contact that business about your information first. If you can't reach them, email us.

Security

Information is encrypted when it travels between the app, our servers and your customers' browsers. Each account can reach only its own data. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and fix problems quickly.

Children

CalenJob is a business tool and isn't meant for children. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change affects how we use information you've already given us, we'll tell you in the app before it takes effect.
