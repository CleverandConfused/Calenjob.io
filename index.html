Features

Everything a contractor calendar needs. Nothing it doesn't.

CalenJob is not bloated field service software. It is a calendar that actually works for people who drive to every job, with quotes and invoices built in.

Automatic drive time

Every job gets a calculated commute from the previous stop, timed with the traffic expected for that day and time. Add your own buffer on top for loading, parking, or just breathing room.

Smart departure alerts

Push notifications remind you when it is time to leave for the next job. Stop running late, stop apologizing to customers.

Mileage and cost tracking

Every trip is logged with distance and the IRS mileage rate or your own per-mile rate. See daily, monthly, and yearly totals for tax deductions and client billing.

Route optimization

Reorder the whole day to minimize total drive time. CalenJob packs your jobs in the smartest order, sets a leave-by time for each one, protects your lunch break, and tells you how many minutes it saved. It works around arrival windows and any jobs you lock in place.

Interactive route map

See your whole day plotted on a map with the drive route drawn between stops. Switch between standard, satellite, and hybrid views.

Recurring schedules

Weekly, every-2-weeks and monthly jobs repeat on their own. Set it once, and CalenJob fills in the calendar.

Quotes customers sign

Build a quote from your saved line items and send it. The customer reviews and signs it from their own phone, and you get notified the moment they do. Follow-up reminders keep open quotes from going cold.

Invoices and receipts

Send an invoice from your phone when the job is done, mark it paid when the money comes in, and send the customer a receipt. No laptop, no second app. Turn a signed quote into an invoice, ask for a deposit, and add a payment link.

Customer Book

Contacts, past and upcoming visits, and notes for every customer, in one place. Optional: the app works fine without it.

Photos and videos on jobs

Keep before and after shots with the job, and add photos to a quote.

Review requests

Text a customer your Google review link with a message you write once.

Bring your calendar with you

Import the calendar you already use from an .ics or .csv file. Drives are added for you.

Automatic backup

Jobs, customers, quotes and photos are backed up to your account on their own. Sign in on a new phone and everything is there.
