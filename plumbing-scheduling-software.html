For Plumbers

Your plumbing schedule looks fine until the drive across town

CalenJob puts the real drive between every service call on your calendar, then reorders the day to cut it. Built for the emergency that wrecks your afternoon.

Four to eight calls, in whatever order the phone rang

A residential plumbing day is typically four to eight service calls, booked in the order they came in rather than the order that makes geographic sense. A water heater in one suburb at nine, a drain clog across the metro at eleven, a fixture swap back near the first job at two.

Then the emergency lands. A burst line at one o'clock does not politely slot into the gap you left, and the rest of the afternoon reshuffles around it. That reshuffle is the moment most scheduling software stops helping, because reordering the day requires a laptop you do not have in the van.

The jobs themselves are predictable. The driving between them is not, and it is the part nobody put on the calendar.

The cost of a bad sequence: Three badly ordered calls across a metro can cost 90 minutes of driving that a different sequence would have cut to 40. At a $115 effective hourly rate, that is most of a billable hour, every day, invisible.

What CalenJob does about it

Real drive time between every stop

Calculated through Google Maps with the traffic expected for the hour the drive actually happens, shown on the timeline with distance and duration.

A leave-by time on every drive

Each drive block says exactly when to walk out the door to arrive on schedule, so you stop doing the arithmetic in your head.

Route optimization from the phone

Reorder the whole day to cut total drive time, standing in a parking lot, and see how many minutes the new order saved.

Arrival windows that hold

Jobs with a fixed window stay inside it while everything else sequences around them, so optimizing does not break your promises.

Works with no signal

A banner tells you service dropped, new jobs get an estimated drive so the day still sequences, and real routes fill in on reconnect.

Mileage without a second app

Every drive is already measured, so the monthly and yearly totals are a byproduct. Export a CSV at tax time.

Questions

Does it handle emergency calls that blow up the schedule?

Yes. Drop the emergency in and re-run the optimizer from your phone, in the van, outside the customer's house. The remaining jobs resequence around it and every leave-by time updates.

What about customers who are only home between nine and eleven?

Set an arrival window on the job and the optimizer will not move it outside that range. Everything else sequences around the fixed points.

Will it work in a crawl space with no signal?

Yes. The app keeps working offline, shows an estimated drive for anything new, and fills in real routes once the phone reconnects.

Does it replace Jobber or Housecall Pro?

For a lot of solo plumbers, yes. It sends quotes, invoices and receipts, and it routes the day, which Jobber saves for its higher plans. What it does not do is take card payments in the app. If you need that today, Jobber for payments plus CalenJob for the route comes to about $58 a month.
